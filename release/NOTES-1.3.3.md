# 1.3.3 — Rune celebrations and readable rewards

**Improved:** each rune reward rolls for four seconds with a slowdown, settle and native chest sounds. Names, ranks and effect descriptions remain browsable with controller or pointer. Newly awarded effects offer **Enable this run / Not now**; duplicate awards use their final rank once. Active runes retain their battle-start rank, capped runes burn into XP inside the popup, and Continue preserves decisions and effects without duplicate rewards.

**Changed:** enabled character runes now work at the start of Endless and after Continue. The old starting-loadout restriction is removed.

**Fixed:** exploration XP uses the native experience pickup and tint; healing uses the actual red-heart pickup, distinct from maximum-health upgrades and soul hearts. Full-health healing and exhausted upgrade chests preview their actual XP replacement, including pointer colour and nearby glow. Stored rolls, reward amounts and boss treasure remain unchanged.

**Updated:** HUD, Custom and achievement reward labels use **Score**. The native results summary shows **Total Score** for the completed run.

Native Windows verification covers actual stat/effect activation, accept/decline, controller/pointer and held-input behaviour, both artwork styles, square/4:3/16:9 layouts, current and public1.3.1 checkpoints, capped conversion and seven exploration rewards. Controlled fixtures establish behaviour, not natural full-run balance. Final exact-package, copied install/rollback and independent public-download evidence is recorded in [PROGRESS.md](../docs/PROGRESS.md).

No new Android gameplay port or monetization is included. Updater0.2.0 remains compatible with schema2/save-epoch1; already transitioned installations keep their saves. Live installations are not changed by publication.
